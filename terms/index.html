Measurements and deployments

The software is under development. Smartphone levels are estimates and have not been validated as standards-compliant measurements. A person operating a service must establish and communicate the applicable service terms, data rights and privacy information for that deployment.

Contributing responsibly

Respect the privacy of people near sensors and avoid publishing recordings, precise private locations or credentials. Contributions and deployment practices should respect the licenses and rights associated with the material used.
